Official task documentation:
https://developers.google.com/edge/mediapipe/solutions/vision/image_segmenter

Input: 256 x 256 RGB. Output: 256 x 256 person confidence.
The model is designed for prominent people at a similar distance from the camera.
Fine hair/fingers, low light, motion, distant people, and differently sized groups
may reduce mask quality. It is not a general object-background remover.

Runtime: @mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.1.1, Apache License 2.0.
The package's unmodified JavaScript and Wasm runtime are copied from node_modules
to public/vendor/mediapipe by scripts/prepare-person-segmentation.mjs at install,
development start, and build. Camera images never leave the local browser for AI
processing; the app serves every AI asset locally and does not use a CDN.
