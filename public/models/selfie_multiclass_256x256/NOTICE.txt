Input: 256x256 RGB. MediaPipe returns six confidence masks:
0 background, 1 hair, 2 body-skin, 3 face-skin, 4 clothes, 5 other accessories.
We calculate person foreground as 1 - background probability. No additional
softmax is applied; MediaPipe handles model metadata and output activation.
Model choice does not guarantee a better result for every scene or person.

Runtime: @mediapipe/tasks-vision 1.1.1, Apache License 2.0. Runtime files are
copied from node_modules to public/vendor/mediapipe at install/dev/build time.
All camera inference and assets remain local to this app.
